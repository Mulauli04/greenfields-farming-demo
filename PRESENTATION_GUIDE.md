# GreenFields — client meeting guide (Ansu Tech Solutions)

## Before the meeting
- Open `presentation.html` using VS Code Live Server. Check that internet access is available for Bootstrap and external photos.
- Use only invented names, emails and phone numbers.
- Verify the browser allows local storage.
- Do not claim GreenFields is a real customer or that the demo is production-ready.

## Suggested 10-minute presentation
1. **0–2 min:** Ask how the farm currently finds buyers and receives enquiries.
2. **2–4 min:** Open `presentation.html`; explain the benefits in simple language.
3. **4–6 min:** Open `products.html`, select a product, request a quote using fictional details.
4. **6–8 min:** Open `admin.html`, load sample enquiries and demonstrate status updates.
5. **8–10 min:** Ask which features matter most and agree on a next meeting to discuss scope and pricing.

## Sample introduction
“You have already built the farming business. My goal is to help more people discover what you sell and contact you easily. This is a sample of what your company website could look like. It does not replace how you farm.”

## Questions to ask
- What products do you sell, and to whom?
- What is your biggest problem with orders or enquiries today?
- Who should be allowed to update products?
- What contact method works best for your customers?
- Who would approve website content and manage it after launch?

## Production work still required
- Confirm actual company identity, brand, products, photographs, prices, contact details and content rights.
- Build server-side authentication, access control and a database; replace browser-only storage.
- Add validation, spam protection, secure hosting, backups and privacy notices/consent where appropriate.
- Implement real enquiry delivery, user testing, accessibility review and ongoing maintenance.
- Agree on scope, cost, timeline and support in writing.
